# Partial Refunds and Restocking Fees

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard procedure for calculating and processing partial refunds for change-of-mind returns. It ensures consistency in applying restocking fees and shipping deductions while maintaining clear communication with Brightcart customers.

## When this applies
This procedure applies to returns initiated within 30 days of delivery where the item is unused and in its original packaging. It specifically covers "change-of-mind" returns, where the customer is responsible for the return label cost and potential restocking fees. This does not apply to defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped items, which are returned free of charge.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Eligibility:** Confirm the return request is within the 30-day window and the item is unused and in its original packaging.
2. **Calculate Deductions:**
   * **Shipping Fee:** Deduct a flat $5.00 for the return label.
   * **Restocking Fee:** If the item is an electronic device and the packaging has been opened, calculate 10% of the item’s purchase price.
   * **Total Deduction:** Add the $5.00 label fee and the 10% restocking fee (if applicable) to determine the total amount to be withheld from the refund.
3. **Process the Return:** Issue the refund for the original purchase price minus the calculated deductions. 
4. **Documentation:** Note the deductions clearly in the ticket internal comments, citing the specific reason for the restocking fee (e.g., "Opened electronics return").
5. **Timeline Notification:** Remind the customer that inspection takes up to 2 business days upon receipt, and the refund will be processed to their card within 5–7 business days thereafter.

## What to tell the customer
"We have processed your return request. Please note that as this is a change-of-mind return, a $5.00 flat fee for the return label and a 10% restocking fee for opened electronics have been deducted from your total refund amount. Once our warehouse receives and inspects your item, your refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days."

## Escalation and priority
* **Priority:** These tickets are categorized as **Medium** priority, as they involve standard financial policy application without immediate urgency.
* **Escalation:** Escalate to a manager only if the customer disputes the restocking fee based on a claim that the item was defective upon arrival or if there is a discrepancy in the original order price. If the refund amount exceeds $200, ensure manager approval is documented in the ticket.

## Common mistakes
* **Forgetting the Label Fee:** Agents occasionally omit the $5.00 flat fee for return labels; ensure this is applied to every change-of-mind return.
* **Incorrect Restocking Scope:** Applying the 10% restocking fee to non-electronic home goods. The 10% fee applies *only* to opened electronics.
* **Vague Communication:** Failing to explicitly state the breakdown of the refund amount in the email to the customer, which often leads to follow-up tickets. Always provide the math clearly.
