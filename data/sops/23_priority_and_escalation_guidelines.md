# Priority and Escalation Guidelines

## Purpose
This document establishes the standard for categorizing and responding to General Inquiry tickets. These guidelines ensure consistent service levels across the Brightcart support team and provide clear escalation paths for time-sensitive issues.

## When this applies
These guidelines apply to all tickets submitted via the web form, email, or live chat. Our support hours are Monday to Saturday, 08:00–20:00. Outside these hours, an on-call agent monitors Urgent tickets.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Categorize:** Assess the ticket content against the Priority Definitions below.
2. **Verify Details:** If the issue involves payment or account access, verify the customer’s identity before discussing specific data.
3. **Set Status:** Apply the correct priority tag in the ticketing system.
4. **Respond:** Address the inquiry within the target timeframe. If an escalation is required, notify the appropriate lead immediately.
5. **Close:** Once the resolution is confirmed or the inquiry answered, close the ticket.

## What to tell the customer
"Thank you for reaching out to Brightcart support. We have received your inquiry and our team is currently reviewing the details. We aim to provide you with a full update regarding your request within [insert target timeframe]."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent (Response: 1 hour):** Money lost (e.g., double charges), account compromise, or site-wide outages (3+ reports in 30 minutes). *Escalation:* Suspected compromise must be escalated to the Security team within 15 minutes. Double charges over $200 require manager approval.
*   **High (Response: 4 hours):** Customer is blocked from completing an order, cannot log in, or reports an item damaged on arrival (within 7 days). *Escalation:* If a promo code failed due to a system fault, escalate to a Team Lead to request authorization to honor it.
*   **Medium (Response: 1 business day):** Inconvenient issues with workarounds, such as standard return requests or inquiries about orders that are not yet "late" (less than 2 days past the estimate).
*   **Low (Response: 2 business days):** General questions, feedback, or requests for information regarding warranty periods (12 months for electronics, 6 months for home goods).

## Common mistakes
*   **Miscalculating "Late" status:** Do not mark a delivery inquiry as "Urgent" unless it is 2 business days past the estimated date. A shipment is only "lost" after 7 business days past the estimate.
*   **Ignoring Security Protocols:** Never change an account’s email or phone number without sending a verification code to the *old* contact method first.
*   **Over-promising Refunds:** Do not promise a refund for a returned item before it has been received and inspected, as inspection takes up to 2 business days and processing to a card takes 5–7 business days.
*   **Mixing up Payment Issues:** Remember that held authorizations for failed payments drop automatically in 3–5 business days; do not open an investigation unless the charge has actually cleared your bank account.
