# Changing Email or Phone Number

## Purpose
This document outlines the mandatory security verification procedure for updating a customer’s registered email address or phone number. These steps are designed to prevent unauthorized account access and protect customer data.

## When this applies
This procedure applies whenever a customer submits a request via the web form to modify the primary contact information associated with their Brightcart account.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Identity:** Confirm the customer's identity by requesting their email address and the order number of their most recent purchase.
2. **Trigger Security Protocol:** Per our account security policy, you must initiate a verification code to be sent to the **old** (current) contact method on file.
3. **Validate Code:** Instruct the customer to provide the code sent to their old email or phone number. Note that these codes are valid for 5 minutes.
4. **Update Details:** Once the code is verified, update the account information in the CRM.
5. **Final Confirmation:** Send a confirmation email to both the old and the new address notifying the user that the account details have been successfully updated.

## What to tell the customer
"For your security, I have sent a verification code to the email address or phone number currently on file. Once you provide that code, I will be able to update your account details immediately."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Low:** Standard requests where the customer has access to their current contact method.
*   **High:** If the customer no longer has access to the old contact method, this becomes a High priority ticket. You must verify their identity through alternative means (such as confirming shipping addresses or recent transaction details) before escalating to a Team Lead for manual override.
*   **Urgent:** If a customer reports they did not request a change and suspects their account has been compromised, treat this as **Urgent**. Immediately lock the account, force a password reset, review the last 30 days of orders, and escalate to the Security team within 15 minutes.

## Common mistakes
*   **Bypassing the Security Code:** Never update contact information without the verification code sent to the existing contact method. Doing so is a direct violation of our account security policy.
*   **Ignoring Suspicious Patterns:** If a customer claims they cannot access their old contact method, do not simply change it. Always perform a thorough identity check to ensure you are not facilitating an account takeover.
*   **Miscalculating Priority:** Failing to recognize a potential compromise as an Urgent security issue. If you suspect the account is being accessed by an unauthorized party, do not wait for the customer to follow up; escalate immediately.
