# Refund Timelines by Payment Method

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard procedure for communicating refund timelines to customers and ensures consistency in how Brightcart handles financial returns across different payment methods.

## When this applies
Use this procedure when a customer submits a ticket via the web form inquiring about the status of a refund, or when an agent has initiated a refund following a successful inspection of a returned item.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Inspection Status:** Check the internal order management system to confirm the returned item has been received and inspected. Remember that inspection takes up to 2 business days from the moment the warehouse receives the parcel.
2. **Identify Payment Method:** Confirm the original payment method used for the order.
3. **Calculate Timeline:**
   * **Store Credit:** Refund is instant upon processing.
   * **Bank Transfer:** Refund takes 3–5 business days.
   * **Card (Credit/Debit):** Refund takes 5–7 business days *after* the inspection process is complete.
4. **Account for Fees:** If the return was a "change-of-mind," ensure the $5.00 label fee (and 10% restocking fee for opened electronics) has been deducted from the total before confirming the amount to the customer.
5. **Respond to Ticket:** Reply to the customer using the guidelines below.

## What to tell the customer
"Once our warehouse completes the inspection of your returned item, which takes up to 2 business days, your refund will be processed immediately. If your original payment was made via credit or debit card, please allow an additional 5–7 business days for the funds to appear in your account. Bank transfers typically reflect within 3–5 business days, while store credit refunds are applied to your account instantly."

## Escalation and priority
* **Low:** General inquiries about refund timelines or status updates when the refund is within the standard processing window.
* **Medium:** If the refund has exceeded the stated timeline (e.g., more than 7 business days for a card refund post-inspection).
* **High:** If the customer is reporting a significant error in the refund amount or if they are unable to access their store credit.
* **Urgent:** If the customer reports a double charge or a missing refund exceeding 14 business days. Escalate to a manager if the refund amount involved exceeds $200.

## Common mistakes
* **Miscalculating the start date:** Remember that the 5–7 business day clock for card refunds only starts *after* the 2-day inspection window, not from the date the customer dropped off the package.
* **Forgetting fees:** Failing to mention the $5.00 return label fee or the 10% restocking fee for opened electronics often leads to follow-up tickets from confused customers.
* **Providing incorrect timelines:** Always distinguish between the processing time for the *refund* and the processing time for the *bank* to post the funds. Ensure you do not promise a specific date, but rather a business day range.
