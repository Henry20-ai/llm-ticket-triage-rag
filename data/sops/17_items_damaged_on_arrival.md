# Items Damaged on Arrival

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard procedure for handling reports of items that arrive in a damaged condition. Our goal is to resolve these issues efficiently while ensuring we adhere to company policy regarding documentation and customer choice.

## When this applies
This procedure applies when a customer contacts Brightcart via the web form to report that an item arrived damaged. Customers must report damage within 7 days of the delivery date. This policy does not cover damage resulting from misuse or wear and tear after arrival.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Eligibility:** Check the order date to ensure the report is within the 7-day window. If the report is outside this window, inform the customer that we cannot process a damage claim, but they may still be eligible for standard warranty coverage.
2. **Request Evidence:** Confirm that the customer has provided clear photos of the damaged item and the original packaging. If photos are missing, request them via email before proceeding.
3. **Verify Documentation:** Review the photos to confirm the damage is consistent with transit issues. 
4. **Offer Resolution:** Ask the customer to choose between a free replacement or a full refund.
5. **Process Request:** 
    *   If **Replacement**: Initiate the shipment of a new item immediately.
    *   If **Refund**: Process the refund to the original payment method. Remind the customer that refunds to a card take 5–7 business days after the return is received and inspected.
6. **Return Logistics:** Provide the customer with a prepaid return label for the damaged item. Ensure they are aware that the item must be returned in its original packaging.

## What to tell the customer
"I am very sorry to hear that your order arrived damaged. Please provide photos of the item and the packaging so we can resolve this for you immediately. Once verified, you may choose between a free replacement or a full refund, and I will provide a prepaid label for the return of the damaged unit."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Priority:** This category is typically **Medium**, as it represents a real problem that requires a specific resolution process.
*   **High:** Escalate to High if the customer is a high-value account or if the item is a critical necessity (e.g., medical-related electronics).
*   **Urgent:** Escalate to Urgent only if the damage involves a safety hazard (e.g., a battery leaking or a fire risk).
*   **Escalation:** If a customer demands compensation beyond a refund or replacement, or if the item value exceeds $500, escalate to a Team Lead.

## Common mistakes
*   **Ignoring the 7-day window:** Failing to check the delivery date before promising a free replacement.
*   **Forgetting the packaging:** Failing to ask for photos of the shipping box, which is necessary for our claims with the courier.
*   **Misleading on timelines:** Promising an "instant" refund to a card. Always specify the 5–7 business day window for cards, noting that store credit is the only instant option.
