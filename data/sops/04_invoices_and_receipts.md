# Invoices and Receipts

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the procedure for handling customer requests regarding billing documentation, including requests for past invoices, digital receipts, and corrections to billing details.

## When this applies
This applies to all tickets categorized under "Billing & Payments" where a customer requests a copy of a receipt, needs a tax invoice for business purposes, or identifies an error in their billing information (such as an incorrect billing address or missing VAT/tax ID).

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Identity:** Confirm the customer’s identity by matching their name, email address, and the last order number provided in the web form.
2. **Locate Documentation:** Access the customer’s order history in the backend system. Retrieve the original invoice or receipt generated at the time of purchase.
3. **Handle Corrections:** 
   * If the customer requests a billing address correction for a past invoice, verify the order status. If the order has already been processed and shipped, inform the customer that the original invoice cannot be retroactively altered for tax purposes.
   * If the request is for a copy of a receipt, send the PDF version directly to the verified email address on file.
4. **Address Double Charges:** If the customer is requesting an invoice because they suspect a double charge, follow the "Double Charges" protocol: verify the transactions within 1 business day. If confirmed, process the refund (up to $200 independently; escalate to a manager for amounts exceeding $200).
5. **Finalize:** Once the action is completed, update the ticket status and ensure the customer is notified.

## What to tell the customer
"I have attached a copy of your invoice for order #[Order Number] to this email for your records. Please note that for security and tax compliance reasons, we are unable to alter billing details on invoices once an order has been processed and shipped. If you have any further questions regarding your payment, please let us know."

## Escalation and priority
* **Low:** General requests for a copy of a receipt or invoice.
* **Medium:** Requests for minor billing detail corrections that do not impact the current order status.
* **High:** If a customer cannot complete a new purchase due to persistent billing errors or if they are missing critical tax documentation required for their own accounting.
* **Urgent:** If the customer reports a suspected double charge or unauthorized transaction. Escalate to a manager immediately if the amount exceeds $200 or if the customer is reporting a potential account compromise.

## Common mistakes
* **Unauthorized Changes:** Attempting to manually edit a generated PDF invoice in the system, which violates accounting integrity.
* **Security Lapses:** Sending billing information to an email address that does not match the one on the original order. Always verify the email against the account on file.
* **Misclassifying Priority:** Treating a confirmed double charge as a "Low" priority request rather than "Urgent," which risks missing the 1-hour response target.
