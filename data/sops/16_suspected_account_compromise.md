# Suspected Account Compromise

## Purpose
This document outlines the mandatory procedure for handling reports of unauthorized account access, unrecognized orders, or suspicious profile changes. The objective is to secure the customer's data immediately, mitigate financial risk, and prevent further unauthorized activity.

## When this applies
Use this procedure when a customer contacts support via the web form, email, or live chat claiming their account has been accessed by a third party. This includes reports of:
*   Unknown orders placed on the account.
*   Unauthorized changes to account email, phone number, or shipping address.
*   Inability to log in despite correct credentials (following the standard 30-minute lockout period).

## Steps for the agent
1.  **Immediate Lockout:** Manually lock the account in the internal dashboard to prevent further unauthorized access.
2.  **Verify Identity:** Confirm the customer’s identity by requesting their email address and the number of their most recent order.
3.  **Force Password Reset:** Trigger a password-reset link to the verified email address. Remind the customer that this link expires in 30 minutes.
4.  **Audit Activity:** Review all account activity from the last 30 days. Specifically, look for shipping address changes, new credit cards added, or orders placed that the customer does not recognize.
5.  **Escalate:** Within 15 minutes of receiving the report, escalate the ticket to the Security team with a summary of the suspicious activity found during your audit.
6.  **Secure Contact Methods:** If the customer reports that their email or phone number was changed without their consent, verify the previous contact method and ensure all future notifications are routed back to the verified owner.

## What to tell the customer
"I have locked your account to prevent any further unauthorized activity and have triggered a secure password-reset link to your email address. Please update your password immediately using that link, as it will expire in 30 minutes. I have escalated this matter to our Security team, who will review your account activity from the last 30 days and reach out to you directly if any fraudulent orders are identified."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Priority:** Suspected account compromise is **always Urgent**. 
*   **Escalation:** All cases must be escalated to the Security team within 15 minutes of initial contact. If the report occurs outside of support hours (08:00–20:00, Monday–Saturday), the on-call agent must be notified immediately to handle the escalation.

## Common mistakes
*   **Failing to lock the account:** Never wait for a supervisor to lock an account; if the customer reports a breach, lock it immediately.
*   **Ignoring the 30-day audit:** Agents often focus only on the most recent order. You must review the last 30 days for any profile changes, as hackers often update contact details to intercept notifications.
*   **Delaying escalation:** Do not attempt to resolve the entire security incident yourself. The 15-minute escalation window is critical to preventing additional financial loss.
