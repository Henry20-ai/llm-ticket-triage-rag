# Failed and Declined Payments

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the procedure for handling customer inquiries regarding payment failures at checkout and inquiries about "held" funds appearing on their bank statements. The goal is to provide clear, accurate information to ensure customers understand that Brightcart has not captured their funds if an order did not complete.

## When this applies
This procedure applies when a customer contacts support via the web form regarding:
*   A "Payment Failed" error message during the checkout process.
*   A "Pending" or "Held" transaction on their bank statement despite the order not being created in the Brightcart system.
*   Confusion regarding why an order was not placed after they attempted to pay.

## Steps for the agent
1.  **Verify the order status:** Search the customer’s email or name in the Brightcart admin panel. Confirm that no order exists for the attempted transaction.
2.  **Confirm payment status:** If the order is missing, the payment did not successfully process through our gateway.
3.  **Explain the "Held" authorization:** If the customer sees a charge, explain that this is a "pending authorization" held by their bank, not a settled charge by Brightcart.
4.  **Advise on resolution:** Inform the customer that the held funds will drop off automatically based on their bank’s processing time, typically 3-5 business days. 
5.  **Troubleshoot:** If the customer wishes to try again, suggest they verify their billing address, check for card expiration, or try an alternative payment method.
6.  **Close the ticket:** Once the explanation is provided, mark the ticket as resolved.

## What to tell the customer
"I have checked our system and can confirm that your order was not successfully placed, which is why you did not receive a confirmation email. The charge you see on your bank statement is a temporary authorization hold that will drop off automatically within 3-5 business days. Please ensure your payment details are correct before attempting the purchase again."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Priority:** This is a **High** priority issue if the customer is currently unable to complete a purchase they are attempting to make. It is **Medium** if the customer is simply inquiring about a past failed attempt.
*   **Escalation:** Escalation is not required for standard payment failures. Escalate to a Team Lead only if the customer provides bank documentation proving the funds were fully captured (settled) despite no order being created, as this indicates a potential system error.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising a refund:** Never tell a customer you are "issuing a refund" for a failed payment. Since Brightcart never captured the funds, there is nothing to refund. Using this language causes confusion when the customer waits for a transaction reversal that is actually just a bank hold expiring.
*   **Ignoring the timeline:** Providing vague timelines for the hold to drop. Always state the specific 3-5 business day window.
*   **Failing to check for double charges:** If a customer reports multiple failed attempts, ensure you check that they were not accidentally charged twice for a *successful* order. If a double charge is confirmed, follow the Double Charges SOP.
