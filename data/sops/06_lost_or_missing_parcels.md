# Lost or Missing Parcels

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the protocol for handling customer reports regarding missing parcels, including those marked as "delivered" but not received, and items that have exceeded the official "lost" threshold.

## When this applies
This procedure applies when a customer contacts us via the web form regarding a missing order. Note that a parcel is officially considered "late" when it is 2 business days past the estimated delivery date and "lost" when it is 7 business days past that date.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Status:** Check the tracking number in the carrier portal. If the status is "Delivered," ask the customer to check with neighbors or building management first.
2. **Confirm Details:** Verify the shipping address provided in the order matches the customer's current address.
3. **Wait Period:** If the parcel is not yet 7 business days past the estimated delivery date, advise the customer to wait, as carriers occasionally mark items as delivered prematurely.
4. **Investigation:** Once the 7-day "lost" threshold is met, open an investigation with the carrier.
5. **Resolution:** If the carrier confirms the package is lost, offer the customer a choice between a full refund or a replacement shipment.
6. **Processing:** If the customer chooses a refund, process it to the original payment method. Remember that refunds to cards take 5-7 business days after processing. If they prefer store credit, this is applied instantly.

## What to tell the customer
"I am very sorry to hear your parcel has not arrived as expected. I have initiated an investigation with the carrier to locate your package, and we will have an update for you within 48 hours. If the parcel is confirmed as lost, we will immediately provide you with a replacement or a full refund, per your preference."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Low:** General inquiries about tracking status before the parcel is officially "late."
*   **Medium:** Confirmed "late" parcels (2 days past estimate).
*   **High:** Confirmed "lost" parcels (7 days past estimate). This requires immediate action to process a refund or replacement.
*   **Urgent:** If the customer reports high-value items or multiple lost orders, or if the customer is expressing significant distress regarding the financial loss.
*   **Escalation:** Escalate to a manager if the total value of the lost items exceeds $200 or if the customer requests a refund method that deviates from our policy.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising instant refunds:** Always remind the customer that card refunds take 5-7 business days to reflect in their account.
*   **Ignoring the 7-day rule:** Do not classify a package as "lost" before it is 7 business days past the estimated delivery date, as many packages arrive shortly after the initial estimate.
*   **Forgetting to check the address:** Always confirm the shipping address before initiating a carrier investigation to ensure the error was not a customer input mistake.
