# Defective Products and Warranty Claims

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the standardized process for handling reports of defective products and warranty claims to ensure consistent service, accurate tracking, and efficient resolution for Brightcart customers.

## When this applies
This procedure is used when a customer reports that an item purchased from Brightcart has stopped working or does not function as described. It covers both "Damaged on Arrival" (DOA) reports and standard warranty claims for electronics and home goods.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Eligibility:** Check the order date in the CRM. Electronics are covered for 12 months from the delivery date; home goods are covered for 6 months.
2. **Assess DOA Status:** If the customer reports damage upon arrival, confirm the report was made within 7 days of delivery. Require photos of the damage and the packaging.
3. **Troubleshoot:** Ask the customer to perform basic troubleshooting steps (e.g., checking power sources, resetting the device). If the issue persists, proceed to the warranty claim.
4. **Issue Return Label:** For verified defective or damaged items, provide a prepaid return shipping label. Do not charge the customer for this return.
5. **Process Resolution:** 
   - **DOA:** Offer the customer a choice between a full refund or a free replacement.
   - **Warranty:** If the product is within the warranty period, facilitate a repair or replacement.
6. **Finalize:** Once the returned item is received, inspect it (within 2 business days). Process the refund or dispatch the replacement immediately following inspection.

## What to tell the customer
"I am very sorry to hear that your product is not working as expected. Please provide photos of the item and its packaging so we can verify the issue and issue a prepaid return label for you. Once we receive and inspect the return, we will prioritize your replacement or full refund."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** If a product defect poses a safety risk (e.g., overheating, fire hazard, or electrical shock), escalate immediately to the Safety and Compliance team.
*   **High:** If the customer is completely blocked from using a critical, high-value item and the situation is time-critical.
*   **Medium:** Standard warranty claims and reports of defective items that do not pose a safety risk.
*   **Low:** General inquiries regarding warranty coverage or minor functional annoyances where the product is still usable.

## Common mistakes
*   **Charging for Returns:** Never charge a customer for a return label if the item is defective or damaged; the $5 fee only applies to change-of-mind returns.
*   **Ignoring Timelines:** Failing to inspect returned items within the 2-business-day window, which delays the customer's refund or replacement.
*   **Incorrect Warranty Windows:** Applying the wrong warranty period (e.g., offering 12 months for home goods instead of the policy-mandated 6 months).
*   **Missing Documentation:** Forgetting to request photos for DOA claims, which is required for our internal quality control records.
