# Wrong or Incomplete Delivery Address

## Purpose
To standardize the process for handling customer requests regarding incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses, ensuring orders are routed correctly while minimizing delivery delays and logistics costs.

## When this applies
This procedure applies when a customer contacts support via the web form to report an error in their shipping information. It covers requests made before an order is dispatched and after the item is already in transit.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Order Status:** Check the order status in the internal dashboard. 
2. **Pre-Dispatch Requests:** If the order is "Pending" or "Processing," update the address in the system immediately. Confirm the change with the customer and ensure the shipping label is regenerated with the correct details.
3. **Post-Dispatch Requests:** If the order has been dispatched, you cannot change the address. Advise the customer that the package will likely be returned to our warehouse as "Undeliverable."
4. **Monitoring:** Once the tracking status updates to "Returned to Sender," notify the customer. Upon receipt and inspection at our warehouse (up to 2 business days), offer the customer the choice to have the item reshipped to the correct address or receive a full refund.
5. **Documentation:** Log all address change requests in the customer’s profile notes, including the date of the request and the outcome.

## What to tell the customer
"I have updated your shipping address in our system to ensure your order reaches you correctly. You will receive a confirmation email shortly with the updated delivery details. If your order has already left our facility, we will need to wait for the courier to return it to us before we can reship it to your correct address."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** Escalated if the incorrect address involves a high-value order (over $500) or if the customer reports the package was delivered to a stranger due to our error.
*   **High:** Use this priority if the order is currently in transit and the customer is concerned about losing the item.
*   **Medium:** Standard address correction requests before dispatch.
*   **Low:** Minor typos (e.g., a missing apartment number) that the courier is likely to resolve without returning the package.
*   **Escalation:** If a package is confirmed "Lost" (7 business days past the estimated delivery date), escalate the ticket to the Logistics Lead for a formal trace.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising delivery dates:** Never guarantee a specific delivery date for a reshipped order, as the return transit process is outside of our control.
*   **Ignoring the 30-day window:** Forgetting that all returns must be processed within 30 days of delivery; if a package is returned after this period, consult a manager.
*   **Manual label overrides:** Attempting to manually force an address change through the courier’s portal instead of our internal system, which causes tracking synchronization errors.
