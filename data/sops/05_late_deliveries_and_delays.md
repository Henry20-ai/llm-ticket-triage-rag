# Late Deliveries and Delays

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for handling customer inquiries regarding delayed shipments. Our goal is to provide accurate tracking information, manage customer expectations, and ensure timely resolution for orders that fail to arrive within the promised timeframe.

## When this applies
This procedure applies to all inquiries submitted via the web form regarding orders that have not arrived by the estimated delivery date. 
*   **Standard Shipping:** 3–5 business days.
*   **Express Shipping:** 1–2 business days.
*   **Definition of Late:** A parcel is considered "late" only once it is 2 business days past its estimated delivery date.
*   **Definition of Lost:** A parcel is considered "lost" if it is 7 business days past its estimated delivery date.

## Steps for the agent
1.  **Verify Status:** Check the tracking number in the logistics portal. Confirm if the parcel is still in transit or if there is a carrier exception (e.g., address issue, weather delay).
2.  **Calculate Eligibility:** Determine if the parcel meets the definition of "late" (2 business days past the estimate). If it is not yet late, politely inform the customer of the current tracking status.
3.  **Investigate:** If the parcel is late, check for carrier updates. If no movement has occurred for 48 hours, contact the carrier’s support desk to initiate an inquiry.
4.  **Take Action:** 
    *   **If Late (but not lost):** Provide the latest carrier update and advise the customer to wait an additional 48 hours.
    *   **If Lost (7+ days late):** Offer the customer a choice between a full refund or a free replacement shipment. 
5.  **Documentation:** Update the ticket with the carrier’s response and the action taken.

## What to tell the customer
"I sincerely apologize for the delay in receiving your order. I have checked the tracking status, and it appears the parcel is currently [status, e.g., in transit/delayed by the carrier]. If your order does not arrive within the next 48 hours, please reach back out to us so we can investigate this as a lost shipment and provide a replacement or a full refund."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Low:** The parcel is delayed by less than 2 business days.
*   **Medium:** The parcel is officially "late" (2+ business days past estimate).
*   **High:** The parcel is officially "lost" (7+ business days past estimate) or the customer has experienced multiple shipping failures.
*   **Escalation:** Escalate to a Team Lead if the customer demands a refund for a non-lost item or if the carrier refuses to acknowledge the loss after 7 days.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising specific delivery times:** Never guarantee a delivery date, as carrier delays are outside our direct control.
*   **Ignoring the 7-day rule:** Do not process a refund for a "lost" package until it has reached the 7-business-day threshold past the estimated date.
*   **Forgetting the tone:** Ensure empathy is used; do not simply copy-paste tracking data without offering an apology for the inconvenience.
