# Store Policies, Hours and Contact Channels

## Purpose
This SOP provides the standard guidelines for handling general customer inquiries regarding Brightcart’s operational policies, shipping, returns, and account security. Adherence ensures consistent messaging and rapid resolution of customer concerns.

## When this applies
This applies to all incoming tickets via the web form, email (support@brightcart.example), or live chat. Use this for questions regarding order status, return eligibility, payment issues, or account access.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Identify the Inquiry:** Categorize the request (e.g., Return, Shipping, Security).
2. **Verify Identity:** For account-related queries, verify identity via email and the last order number.
3. **Check Policy:** Consult the specific policy facts (e.g., return windows, restocking fees, or warranty periods).
4. **Take Action:** 
   - For **returns**, assess if the item is within 30 days and unused.
   - For **damaged items**, ensure photos are provided within 7 days.
   - For **double charges**, verify the duplicate within 1 business day. If under $200, process the refund; if over $200, escalate to a manager.
5. **Set Expectations:** Clearly communicate timelines for refunds (5-7 days for cards, instant for store credit) or shipping (3-5 business days standard).
6. **Log Interaction:** Ensure the ticket is tagged correctly and notes are added to the CRM.

## What to tell the customer
"Thank you for reaching out to Brightcart. I have reviewed your request and confirmed that your item is eligible for a return under our 30-day policy. Please note that for change-of-mind returns, a $5 label fee will be deducted from your refund once we receive and inspect the item."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** Security compromises, suspected fraud, or double charges. Escalate suspected compromises to the Security team within 15 minutes.
*   **High:** Payment failures or inability to place orders.
*   **Medium:** Returns, warranty claims, or "late" parcels (2 days past estimate).
*   **Low:** General questions, feedback, or minor website annoyances. 
*   **Outages:** If >3 customers report the same issue within 30 minutes, mark as **Urgent** and notify Engineering on-call.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising immediate refunds:** Always specify the 5-7 business day window for cards to avoid follow-up tickets.
*   **Ignoring the 10% restocking fee:** Failing to mention this for opened electronics leads to customer dissatisfaction during the refund process.
*   **Over-promising on promo codes:** Never apply expired codes unless a system fault is confirmed by a Team Lead.
*   **Miscalculating "Lost" status:** Do not classify a parcel as "lost" until it is 7 business days past the estimated delivery date.
*   **Security bypass:** Never change an account email or phone number without sending a verification code to the *old* contact method.
