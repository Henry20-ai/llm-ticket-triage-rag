# Handling a Double Charge

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the procedure for investigating and resolving reports of duplicate charges on customer accounts to ensure financial accuracy and maintain customer trust.

## When this applies
This applies when a customer contacts support via the web form claiming that the same order has been charged to their payment method twice. This procedure is specifically for confirmed duplicate transactions; it does not apply to "held" authorizations for failed or declined payments, which drop automatically within 3–5 business days.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify the claim:** Within 1 business day of receiving the ticket, check the order history and payment logs in the admin dashboard. Confirm if two distinct transaction IDs exist for the same order number.
2. **Distinguish from holds:** If the duplicate is actually a pending authorization from a failed payment attempt, explain that the hold will drop automatically within 3–5 business days. No refund action is required.
3. **Initiate refund:** If a duplicate charge is confirmed, process a full refund for the second transaction.
4. **Seek approval:** If the duplicate charge amount is $200.00 or less, you may process the refund independently. If the amount exceeds $200.00, you must escalate the ticket to a manager for approval before issuing the refund.
5. **Timeline:** Once confirmed, ensure the refund is processed so that it reaches the customer’s card within 5 business days.

## What to tell the customer
"I have investigated your account and confirmed that a duplicate charge occurred for your order. I have initiated a full refund for the second transaction, which should appear on your original payment method within 5 business days. We apologize for this technical error and any inconvenience it may have caused."

## Escalation and priority
* **Priority:** This ticket must be marked as **Urgent** because it involves money lost or at risk.
* **Escalation:** If the duplicate charge exceeds $200.00, you must escalate the ticket to a manager for approval. If you are working outside of standard support hours (08:00–20:00, Monday to Saturday), please flag the ticket for the on-call agent to handle immediately upon their review.

## Common mistakes
* **Confusing holds with charges:** Frequently, customers see a "pending" authorization from a failed checkout attempt and mistake it for a double charge. Always check the payment status; if the order was never successfully placed, the payment was never captured.
* **Delayed verification:** Failing to verify the claim within the 1-business-day window, which risks customer dissatisfaction.
* **Unauthorized refunds:** Processing a refund over $200.00 without obtaining the required manager approval, which violates financial oversight protocols.
* **Miscalculating the refund timeline:** Providing incorrect estimates to the customer. Always state that the refund will be processed within 5 business days, distinct from the 5–7 business day window used for standard return-based refunds.
