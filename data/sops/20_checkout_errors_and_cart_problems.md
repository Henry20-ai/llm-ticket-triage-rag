# Checkout Errors and Cart Problems

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the protocol for resolving technical issues preventing customers from completing purchases on the Brightcart website or app. Our goal is to minimize friction, protect revenue, and ensure a seamless shopping experience.

## When this applies
Use this guide when a customer reports:
*   Checkout page failing to load or process payment.
*   Items disappearing from the cart unexpectedly.
*   Buttons (e.g., "Add to Cart," "Proceed to Checkout") being unresponsive.
*   Payment gateway errors or persistent "Failed" transaction messages.

## Steps for the agent
1.  **Verify the issue:** Ask the customer for their browser/device type and a screenshot of the error.
2.  **Basic Troubleshooting:** Advise the customer to clear their browser cache, try an incognito window, or switch to a different device/browser.
3.  **Check for Outages:** If more than 3 customers report the same issue within 30 minutes, treat this as a potential system outage.
4.  **Verify Payment Status:** If the customer reports a "failed" payment, explain that the order was not placed. Confirm if a temporary authorization hold appears on their statement; inform them this will drop automatically in 3-5 business days.
5.  **Promo Code Issues:** If a code failed, verify if it is expired. If the code is valid but failed due to a system fault, document the issue and request approval from a Team Lead to honour it.
6.  **Documentation:** Log all steps taken in the ticket. If the issue remains unresolved, escalate to the Technical Support queue.

## What to tell the customer
"I am sorry for the trouble you’re experiencing with your cart. Please try clearing your browser cache or using an incognito window, as this often resolves temporary connectivity issues. If the problem persists, please send us a screenshot of the error so our technical team can investigate the root cause immediately."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** If more than 3 customers report the same issue within 30 minutes, mark the ticket **Urgent**, notify the Engineering on-call team, and escalate. Also **Urgent** if a customer reports a double charge or suspected account compromise.
*   **High:** If the customer is completely blocked from checking out and manual troubleshooting has failed.
*   **Medium:** If the checkout works intermittently or requires a specific workaround (e.g., using a different browser).
*   **Low:** Minor display glitches that do not prevent the actual purchase.

## Common mistakes
*   **Ignoring Outages:** Failing to report a pattern of multiple tickets to the Engineering team, leading to prolonged downtime.
*   **Misleading on Refunds:** Promising an immediate refund for a "failed" payment. Remember: failed payments do not require refunds; they are simply authorization holds that expire in 3-5 business days.
*   **Ignoring Escalation Timelines:** Failing to escalate to the Security team within 15 minutes for suspected account compromises.
