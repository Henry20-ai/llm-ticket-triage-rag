# Issuing Return Labels

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the procedure for generating and issuing prepaid return shipping labels to customers. It ensures consistency in handling return fees, restocking charges, and communication regarding Brightcart’s return policy.

## When this applies
This process applies when a customer requests a return for an item purchased within the last 30 days. It covers both defective/incorrect items (free returns) and change-of-mind returns (subject to fees).

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Eligibility:** Check the order date to ensure the request is within the 30-day window. Confirm the item is unused and in its original packaging.
2. **Categorize the Return:**
    * **Defective/Damaged/Wrong Item:** No fees apply. Proceed to step 3.
    * **Change-of-Mind:** Apply a flat $5 label fee. If the item is an opened electronic device, add a 10% restocking fee. Both will be deducted from the final refund.
3. **Generate Label:** Use the Internal Logistics Portal to generate a prepaid shipping label associated with the order number.
4. **Attach and Send:** Download the PDF label and attach it to the support ticket response.
5. **Update Ticket:** Add a private note to the ticket detailing the reason for the return and any applicable fees deducted, then set the status to "Pending" until the item is received and inspected.

## What to tell the customer
"I have attached your prepaid return label to this email. Please note that for change-of-mind returns, a $5 label fee will be deducted from your refund, along with a 10% restocking fee for opened electronics. Once our warehouse receives and inspects your item—which takes up to 2 business days—we will process your refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days."

## Escalation and priority
* **Priority:** This is generally a **Medium** priority ticket.
* **Urgent:** If the customer is reporting a return issue related to a security breach or a major financial error (e.g., they were charged for a return they never initiated), escalate to **Urgent**.
* **Escalation:** Escalate to a Team Lead if the customer disputes the restocking fee or if you are unsure about the condition of the returned item upon inspection.

## Common mistakes
* **Forgetting the restocking fee:** Always check if the item is an electronic device before confirming the refund amount to the customer.
* **Incorrect fee calculation:** Ensure the $5 label fee is applied to all change-of-mind returns, regardless of the item size or value.
* **Setting wrong expectations:** Never promise an "instant" refund to a card; always specify the 5-7 business day window after the 2-day inspection period.
* **Missing the 30-day window:** Do not issue a label for items delivered more than 30 days ago without consulting a supervisor.
