# Password Resets and Locked Accounts

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the standard procedure for assisting Brightcart customers who are unable to access their accounts, ensuring security and efficiency while maintaining our service level agreements.

## When this applies
This applies to all tickets categorized under "Account & Access," specifically:
*   Customers who are locked out due to failed login attempts.
*   Issues regarding expired or non-functional password reset links.
*   Requests to update account credentials (email/phone).
*   Reports of unauthorized account activity or suspected compromise.

## Steps for the agent
1.  **Verify Identity:** For all account access requests, verify the customer’s identity by confirming their email address and the number of their most recent order.
2.  **Handle Lockouts:** If a customer is locked out, inform them that the system automatically unlocks after 30 minutes. Do not manually override the lockout timer.
3.  **Process Reset Links:** If a user reports a broken reset link, confirm the link has expired (links expire after 30 minutes). Generate a new request via the admin panel if the customer is still unable to receive the automated email.
4.  **Credential Changes:** If a customer needs to change their email or phone number, explain that a verification code must be sent to their *old* contact method first. If they no longer have access to the old method, escalate to a supervisor.
5.  **Compromise Protocol:** If a customer suspects their account was hacked, follow the "Suspected Account Compromise" protocol: immediately lock the account, force a password reset, review the last 30 days of order activity, and escalate to the Security team within 15 minutes.

## What to tell the customer
"For your security, our system automatically locks accounts for 30 minutes after five failed login attempts; please wait until this period has passed before trying again. If you need to reset your password, please use the link sent to your email within 30 minutes, as it will expire after that time. If you suspect unauthorized access, please let us know immediately so we can secure your account."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** Suspected account compromise or unauthorized orders. Escalate to the Security team within 15 minutes.
*   **High:** Customer is unable to log in to complete a time-sensitive purchase or manage an existing order.
*   **Medium:** General password reset issues or difficulties navigating the account recovery flow.
*   **Low:** General inquiries about account settings or profile updates that do not prevent login.

## Common mistakes
*   **Manual Overrides:** Attempting to force-unlock an account before the 30-minute timer expires.
*   **Identity Negligence:** Failing to verify the last order number before providing account assistance.
*   **Ignoring Security:** Treating a report of a "hacked account" as a standard login issue rather than an Urgent security escalation.
*   **Mismanaging Outages:** Failing to report repeated login failures; remember that if more than 3 customers report the same issue within 30 minutes, it must be escalated as a potential outage.
