# Customer Communication Standards

## Purpose
This document outlines the communication standards for the Brightcart support team. Our goal is to provide consistent, empathetic, and accurate information to ensure every customer feels heard and supported.

## When this applies
These standards apply to all General Inquiry tickets submitted via our web form, email, or live chat. Agents must adhere to these guidelines to maintain brand integrity and operational efficiency.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Acknowledge and Validate:** Always start by acknowledging the customer's specific concern. If a mistake occurred, offer a sincere apology without over-explaining.
2. **Verify Details:** Check the account status and order history before responding. If a return or refund is requested, confirm the eligibility (e.g., 30-day window for returns) based on the delivery date.
3. **Provide Clear Expectations:** Use the defined timelines (e.g., 5-7 business days for card refunds, 3-5 business days for standard delivery). Never promise a specific outcome that requires management approval or technical intervention until it is confirmed.
4. **Close the Ticket:** Once the resolution is provided, ask if the customer has any further questions. If the issue is resolved, use the standard closing: "Thank you for choosing Brightcart. Please feel free to reach out if you need further assistance."

## What to tell the customer
"I sincerely apologize for the delay in receiving your order. Since your parcel is now two business days past the estimated delivery date, I have opened an investigation with our carrier. I will provide you with an update within one business day."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Urgent:** Issues involving suspected account compromise, active double charges, or system-wide outages (3+ reports in 30 minutes). Escalate to Security or Engineering immediately.
*   **High:** Issues preventing a customer from completing a purchase or logging in. Response target: 4 hours.
*   **Medium:** Real problems with an inconvenient workaround, such as a delayed parcel that is not yet "lost." Response target: 1 business day.
*   **Low:** General product questions or feedback. Response target: 2 business days.

*Note:* If a refund request exceeds $200, you must escalate to a manager for approval before confirming the transaction.

## Common mistakes
*   **Promising outcomes:** Never guarantee a refund or a specific delivery date before checking internal systems.
*   **Vague timelines:** Avoid saying "soon" or "shortly." Always use the specific policy numbers (e.g., "5-7 business days for card refunds").
*   **Ignoring policy:** Do not offer to waive the $5 label fee for change-of-mind returns or the 10% restocking fee for opened electronics without explicit management authorization.
*   **Tone inconsistency:** Avoid overly casual language. Maintain a professional, helpful tone even when the customer is frustrated.
