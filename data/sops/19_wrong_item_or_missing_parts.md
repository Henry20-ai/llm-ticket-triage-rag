# Wrong Item or Missing Parts

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the standardized process for handling customer reports regarding incorrect items received or incomplete shipments. Our goal is to resolve these issues efficiently while ensuring accurate documentation for inventory tracking and warehouse quality control.

## When this applies
This procedure applies to all "Product Issue" tickets where a customer claims they received a product different from their order confirmation or an item that is missing specific components (e.g., cables, manuals, or modular parts). 

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Order Details:** Locate the order in the internal dashboard. Confirm the items listed on the invoice against the customer’s report.
2. **Request Evidence:** Ask the customer to provide a photo of the item received, the original packaging, and the shipping label. This is mandatory for investigating warehouse errors.
3. **Check Inventory/Stock:** If the correct item is in stock, offer a replacement. If the item is out of stock, offer a full refund.
4. **Initiate Return:** For wrong items, provide a prepaid return label. Remind the customer that the item must be returned unused and in its original packaging.
5. **Missing Parts:** If only a small part is missing, check if we can ship the component separately. If not, follow the standard return process for the full item.
6. **Finalize:** Once the return is received and inspected (up to 2 business days), process the refund to the original payment method (5–7 business days) or issue instant store credit per the customer's preference.

## What to tell the customer
"I am very sorry for the frustration caused by receiving the incorrect item. I have generated a prepaid return label for you, and we will ship your correct item immediately once the return is scanned by the carrier. Please ensure the item remains unused and in its original packaging to ensure a smooth return process."

## Escalation and priority
*   **Low:** Minor missing accessory that does not prevent the core use of the product.
*   **Medium:** Incorrect item received or critical missing parts that prevent the product from functioning.
*   **High:** The customer has a time-sensitive need for the item (e.g., a gift or professional use) and the error prevents them from completing their task.
*   **Urgent:** If the customer has been sent an item of significantly higher value or if the error involves a high-value electronics order that has been misdelivered or compromised, escalate to a team lead. 
*   **Escalation:** Escalate to a manager if the customer requests a refund exceeding $200 or if the customer is expressing extreme dissatisfaction that requires a goodwill gesture beyond standard policy.

## Common mistakes
*   **Forgetting the Label:** Failing to provide the prepaid label for wrong items, which violates our policy of free returns for company errors.
*   **Premature Refunds:** Issuing a refund before the warehouse has inspected the returned item.
*   **Ignoring Inspection:** Skipping the 2-day inspection period, which can lead to processing returns for damaged or used goods that are ineligible for a full refund.
*   **Vague Communication:** Failing to mention the 30-day return window or the specific timeline for card refunds.
