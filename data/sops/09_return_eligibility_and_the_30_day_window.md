# Return Eligibility and the 30-Day Window

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the standard procedure for handling return requests at Brightcart. Ensuring consistent application of our 30-day window and fee structure maintains financial accuracy and customer trust.

## When this applies
This policy applies to all customer-initiated return requests submitted via our web form. It covers all electronics and home goods delivered nationwide. Returns are eligible only if the request is made within 30 days of the delivery date and the item remains unused in its original packaging.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Eligibility:** Confirm the delivery date in the order management system. If the request is beyond the 30-day window, deny the return unless the item is defective (refer to Warranty policy).
2. **Determine Return Category:**
   * **Defective/Damaged/Wrong Item:** Issue a prepaid return label at no cost to the customer.
   * **Change-of-Mind:** Apply a flat $5.00 label fee. If the item is an opened electronic, additionally calculate and note a 10% restocking fee.
3. **Process the Label:** Generate the return label through the shipping portal and email it to the customer.
4. **Explain Refund Timeline:** Inform the customer that inspection takes up to 2 business days upon receipt. Once approved, refunds to a card take 5-7 business days, bank transfers take 3-5 business days, and store credit is applied instantly.
5. **Update Ticket:** Log the return type and fees applied in the ticket notes.

## What to tell the customer
"I have initiated your return request, and you will receive a prepaid shipping label via email shortly. Please note that for change-of-mind returns, a $5.00 label fee (and a 10% restocking fee for opened electronics) will be deducted from your final refund. Once we receive and inspect your package, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5-7 business days."

## Escalation and priority
* **Low:** Standard change-of-mind returns or general inquiries about the return policy.
* **Medium:** Disputes regarding the condition of the item or the application of the restocking fee.
* **High:** If a customer insists on a return outside the 30-day window due to an alleged system error or shipping delay.
* **Urgent:** If a customer claims they were charged multiple times for a single return or if the return involves a high-value item (>$500) that was lost in transit.
* **Escalation:** Escalate to a Team Lead if the customer demands a fee waiver that exceeds your authority or if the return involves a complex warranty dispute.

## Common mistakes
* **Forgetting the Restocking Fee:** Always check if the item is an "electronic" before finalizing the label; failing to apply the 10% fee for opened electronics is a common financial error.
* **Miscalculating the Window:** Ensure you count 30 days from the *delivery* date, not the order date.
* **Ignoring Condition:** Failing to confirm that the item is in its "original packaging" before authorizing the return.
