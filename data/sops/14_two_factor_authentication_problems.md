# Two-Factor Authentication Problems

## Purpose
This SOP outlines the procedure for assisting customers who are unable to access their Brightcart accounts due to issues with Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). Our goal is to balance account security with efficient customer access.

## When this applies
Use this procedure when a customer reports:
* 2FA codes not arriving via SMS or email.
* 2FA codes showing as expired (codes are valid for 5 minutes).
* The loss of the device used for 2FA.
* The need to reset 2FA settings due to a change in phone number or email.

## Steps for the agent
1. **Verify Identity:** Before altering any security settings, you must verify the customer's identity. Request the account email address and the order number of their most recent purchase.
2. **Troubleshoot Codes:** If the customer reports codes not arriving, advise them to check their spam folder (for email) or confirm their carrier is not blocking short-code SMS. Remind them that codes expire after 5 minutes.
3. **Handle Lost Devices/Contact Changes:** If the customer cannot access their old phone or email, explain that for security, changes require a code sent to the *old* contact method. If they have no access to the old method, proceed to manual verification.
4. **Resetting 2FA:** Once verified, you may reset the 2FA settings. This allows the customer to re-enroll with a new device or contact method upon their next login.
5. **Account Lockout:** Be aware that accounts lock for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. If a customer is locked out, inform them they must wait the full 30 minutes before attempting to log in again.

## What to tell the customer
"I understand you are having trouble accessing your account, and I would be happy to help you regain access. To ensure your account security, could you please provide the email address associated with your account and your most recent order number? Once I verify these details, I can assist you with resetting your 2FA settings."

## Escalation and priority
* **Urgent:** If a customer reports unauthorized activity or suspected account compromise, this is an **Urgent** ticket. You must lock the account, force a password reset, review the last 30 days of orders, and escalate to the Security team within 15 minutes.
* **High:** If the customer is completely blocked from their account and unable to complete a purchase, this is a **High** priority ticket (4-hour response target).
* **Medium:** General 2FA technical issues that do not prevent a time-sensitive purchase are **Medium** priority (1-business-day response target).

## Common mistakes
* **Skipping Identity Verification:** Never bypass the requirement for the email address and last order number. Failure to verify leads to potential account takeovers.
* **Ignoring Security Protocols:** Do not attempt to change an email or phone number without first attempting to send the verification code to the existing, registered contact method.
* **Mismanaging Urgency:** Failing to escalate suspected compromises to the Security team within the 15-minute window is a serious policy violation.
